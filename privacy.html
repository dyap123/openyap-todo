OpenYap Todo

Privacy

Updated September 29, 2026

OpenYap Todo helps you organize tasks, projects, categories, mind maps and schedules. This page explains how this app handles account and calendar information.

Your account and workspace

Firebase Authentication handles email/password and Google sign-in. Account information can include your email address, display name, profile photo and unique account identifier. Your tasks, descriptions, project and category settings, mind maps, dates and completion history are stored in the dedicated OpenYap Todo Firebase project. Workspace access is restricted by account through database rules. This app's database is separate from the OpenYap Command Center database.

Google Calendar

Calendar access is optional and requested separately from signing in. If you connect, the app reads events from your primary calendar to show their dates, titles, descriptions, locations, organizers, attendees and available meeting links. Calendar event data and Calendar access tokens are kept in the current page's memory. This app does not save them to its Firebase database or browser storage. They are cleared when you disconnect, sign out, change accounts, reload or close the page, or the token expires.

The app requests read-only Calendar access. Meeting buttons open the relevant meeting provider. Invite buttons open a prefilled Google Calendar page, passing the selected event's title, dates, plain-text description and location to Google; you review it, add guests and send from Google Calendar. Todo does not send invitations itself.

Storage and service providers

Firebase and Google process information necessary to provide authentication, workspace storage and Calendar access. GitHub Pages serves the app. Your browser retains interface preferences and may cache the app's static files for installation. The app does not cache your workspace records or Calendar responses for offline use. Firebase may retain your sign-in session on the device until you sign out.

This app does not include advertising or sell Calendar data. Exporting a report, Markdown document or other download creates a file under your control; sharing that file shares its contents with the recipient.

Your controls

You can edit or delete workspace content in the app. Calendar Disconnect clears this tab's connection; you can also remove the app's access from your Google Account connections. Sign out when using a shared device. Account deletion and complete workspace removal currently require help from the app operator; use the support contact displayed on the app's Google consent screen. Removing Google access alone does not delete your Todo workspace.
